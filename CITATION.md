# Citing COMPSs

If you use **COMPSs** in your research, publications, or projects, please cite the appropriate references listed below.

> Depending on which COMPSs component or functionality you use, please refer to the corresponding publication.

## Primary citation

Lordan, F., Tejedor, E., Ejarque, J., Rafanell, R., Álvarez, J., Marozzo, F., Lezzi, D., Sirvent, R., Talia, D., & Badia, R. M. (2014).

**ServiceSs: An interoperable programming framework for the Cloud.**

*Journal of Grid Computing*, 12(1), 67–91.

[https://doi.org/10.1007/s10723-013-9272-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10723-013-9272-5)
